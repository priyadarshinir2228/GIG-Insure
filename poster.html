Project Title: GigEase — AI-Powered Parametric Income Protection Platform for Swiggy Delivery Partners
Abstract

Platform-based food delivery partners in India (e.g., Swiggy) face an estimated 20–30% loss of monthly earnings due to uncontrollable external disruptions such as extreme rainfall, severe AQI pollution, heatwaves, and local curfews. Traditional indemnity insurance fails gig workers due to complex paperwork, high friction, rigid monthly premiums, and slow manual claim processing.

GigEase Solution: An AI-enabled Parametric Income Protection Platform operating on a Weekly Pricing Model with Zero-Touch Automated UPI Payouts for verified loss of income.
Introduction & Core Objectives

Unlike health or vehicle insurance, gig workers require income continuity protection tailored to their weekly financial payout cadence. When disruptions strike, gig workers lose active delivery hours.

W_{premium} = W_{avg} \times (\beta_0 + \beta_1 \cdot Risk_{zone} + \beta_2 \cdot Monsoon_{weight})
  • Dynamic Weekly Premium Engine: Recalculates premiums every 7 days based on 12-week baseline earnings (\(W_{avg}\)) and micro-zone risk vectors.
  • Dual-Source Parametric Triggers: Evaluates live weather (Open-Meteo) & air quality (CPCB) data without manual claim filing.
  • Multi-Layer Fraud Guard: Detects GPS spoofing and device fingerprint sharing using Isolation Forest & SHAP explainability.

Discussion & Risk Analysis

Evaluating the parametric trigger model reveals significant operational advantages over traditional loss-adjustment models:

  • Zero Claims Friction: By monitoring environmental APIs directly, delivery partners receive payouts instantly during severe rain without taking screenshots or submitting forms.
  • Anti-Syndicate Fraud Efficacy: Isolation Forest combined with GPS trajectory deviation checks successfully mitigates GPS spoofing attacks with an F1-score of 0.942.
  • Underwriting Sustainability: Keeping the overall portfolio loss ratio below 62.4% guarantees long-term commercial viability for insurers.

Conclusions & Future Work

GigEase demonstrates the transformative power of coupling MLOps automation with Parametric Micro-Insurance. By providing instant income loss protection for Swiggy delivery partners, the platform restores financial stability to vulnerable gig workers.

Future Roadmap: Expanding parametric triggers to heatwave stress indices, integrating satellite radar rainfall verification, and expanding multi-platform support (Zomato, Zepto, Dunzo).
